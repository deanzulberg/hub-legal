Hub Privacy Policy

Hub is a personal app used only by its owner.

What Hub accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Hub reads and manages the owner's own Google Calendar data (and, where enabled, reads their Gmail messages) so it can show them inside the app.

How data is handled

  • Data is fetched directly from Google to the owner's own device and is not sent to any server operated by Hub.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage.
  • Hub does not sell, share, or transfer this data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • Hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Hub's access at any time in their Google Account under Security, then Third-party access.
